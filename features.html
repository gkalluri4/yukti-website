Every feature a restaurant CPA needs, nothing they don't

From statement upload to posted journal entry to filed tax return — every step automated, every number verified

Automated statement parsing

Upload a statement — PDF, Excel, or CSV. Get structured financial data in seconds.

Platform auto-detection

Drop any restaurant statement — PDF, Excel, or CSV. Yukti identifies the platform automatically from the content. DoorDash PDF, Toast Excel, Clover CSV — all recognized instantly.

Every field extracted

Revenue, commissions, marketing fees, processing fees, error charges, tax collected, marketplace facilitator status, tips, gratuity, cash and credit breakdowns — nothing missed.

AI for understanding, code for math

AI reads and understands PDF statements. But every financial calculation — every dollar, every cent — is deterministic code. No AI in the math. Numbers you can audit and verify.

Delivery platforms and POS systems

Both sides of restaurant revenue — what comes through delivery apps and what comes through the register

Delivery platforms

DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub monthly statement PDFs. Each platform has its own parser, its own rules, its own configuration. Revenue, commissions, marketing fees, error charges, and net payout — all extracted and balanced.

POS systems

Toast (Sales Summary Excel + Processing Statement PDF) and Clover (Sales Report CSV). In-house sales, tax, tips, cash and credit card breakdowns, processing fees — separated from delivery platform orders to avoid double-counting.

Extensible by design

Each platform is an isolated parser with its own rule configuration. Adding a new delivery platform or POS system follows a standard checklist — no architectural changes, no risk to existing platforms.

One dashboard for the complete picture

Every revenue source, every fee, every tax dollar — unified in one view

Revenue flow per source

For each platform: gross sales, commissions, marketing fees, adjustments, withholdings, net receivable, tips, tax owed to CDTFA, and net after tax and tips. One row per source, everything visible.

Monthly and quarterly views

Toggle between monthly detail and quarterly summary. The quarterly view feeds directly into your CDTFA filing — same numbers, verified against each other.

Costs highlighted visually

Platform commissions, marketing fees, and withholdings are highlighted as costs. Tax is shown as pass-through (collected from customers, remitted to the state). Tips are shown as pass-through to staff. The bottom line shows what the restaurant actually keeps.

California tax compliance built in

Marketplace facilitator rules, CDTFA liability calculation, 401-EZ filing worksheet

Marketplace facilitator rules

Yukti knows that Uber Eats remits sales tax in California but DoorDash and Grubhub don't. The correct tax treatment per platform, computed automatically. No manual lookup, no mistakes.

CDTFA 401-EZ filing worksheet

Your quarterly California sales tax return, pre-filled from Yukti data. Total Sales, marketplace facilitator deductions, taxable amount, state tax, district tax, Schedule A2, Schedule C — all computed. Download as Excel, review, file. A 2-4 hour manual task becomes a 10-minute review.

Tax rate validation

Cross-checks your effective tax rate against the California Tax Rate API for your restaurant's address. Catches discrepancies — like the $82/month tax overstatement we discovered at a real restaurant — before they become problems.

Works with any accounting system

Post directly to QuickBooks or download — your choice

QuickBooks Online API

One-click posting with idempotency protection. OAuth 2.0, encrypted tokens, automatic refresh. Separate A/R accounts per platform so the CPA can reconcile each platform's deposits independently.

Download as Excel, IIF, or CSV

Excel for universal use with full audit trail. IIF for QuickBooks Desktop. Xero-compatible CSV for Xero. Professional formatting with every line showing exactly which statement fields produced the number.

Monthly package download

One Excel file with all journal entries for the month — summary sheet, line details, and tax summary. Hand it to any bookkeeper — everything they need in one file.

Security your clients' data deserves

Financial-grade security at every layer

AES-256 encryption

QuickBooks OAuth tokens encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Credentials never stored in plain text, never logged, never exposed.

8-year data retention

Statements and journal entries retained for 8 years per IRS and CDTFA requirements. Your audit trail is always available when you need it.

Role-based access

Restaurant owners control access. CPAs get scoped access to only their clients. Invitation-based onboarding. Revocation in one click. CCPA compliant.
