Common questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Start with a free trial — no credit card required. Upload real statements from any supported platform and see balanced journal entries before committing to a paid plan. Your data stays intact when you upgrade.

Do I need QuickBooks to use Yukti?

No. Yukti generates balanced journal entries you can download as Excel (universal), IIF (QuickBooks Desktop), or CSV (Xero). The QuickBooks Online API integration is included but not required. Yukti works with any accounting system.

What platforms are supported?

DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub for delivery platforms. Toast and Clover for POS systems. All five platforms are included in every plan — no per-platform charges. New platforms are added regularly.

What if I have more than 10 statements per month?

Most restaurants generate 5-8 statements per month (one per platform per month, plus POS processing statements). The 10-statement limit covers the vast majority of cases. Contact us if you need more.

Does Yukti replace my accountant?

No — and it isn't meant to. Yukti reconciles your platform statements into balanced, review-ready journal entries; your CPA reviews and approves every entry before it reaches the books. Yukti makes your accountant faster, not redundant.
