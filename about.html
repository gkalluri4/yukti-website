Built by a restaurant owner, for restaurant owners and their CPAs

Yukti was born from a real problem at a real restaurant in Anaheim, California

The problem we lived

Every month, our restaurant received statements from DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, and our Clover POS — each with different formats, different fee structures, and different tax treatments. Converting these into correct journal entries took our CPA 4-10 hours per restaurant per month.

Worse, we discovered we were overpaying sales tax by $82 every month because the POS was inflating tax calculations on delivery orders with platform-funded discounts. That's over $1,000 per year in overpaid tax — from a single platform, at a single restaurant.

We also discovered that California's marketplace facilitator rules meant Uber Eats was remitting tax on our behalf, but DoorDash and Grubhub weren't. Without knowing this, we were either double-paying tax on Uber Eats orders or under-paying on DoorDash orders — depending on how our CPA filed.

We looked for a solution. Enterprise tools cost $500-2,000/month and required implementation teams. Nothing existed for independent restaurants and their CPAs. So we built Yukti.

Real restaurant, real data

Every feature was built and tested against real monthly statements from a real restaurant — not synthetic test data. Five platforms, four months, every number verified to the penny.

Accounting standards first

USAR-aligned chart of accounts. CDTFA marketplace facilitator rules. Separate A/R per platform for proper bank reconciliation. Industry-standard from day one.

Financial-grade security

AES-256 encryption, OAuth with auto-refresh, 8-year data retention, rate limiting, CCPA compliant. Your data is protected.

What "Yukti" means

Yukti is a Sanskrit word meaning "skillful method" — the art of applying the right technique to the right problem. In ancient Indian philosophy, yukti represents intelligent reasoning that transforms complexity into clarity.

That's exactly what our platform does. It takes the complexity of multi-platform restaurant revenue — different formats, different fees, different tax rules across DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Toast, and Clover — and applies a skillful method to produce clarity: balanced, verified, audit-ready journal entries.

Every restaurant's revenue flows through different channels. Yukti is the skillful method that brings them all together.

Our approach

Three principles that guide every design decision

AI for understanding, code for math

We use AI to read and understand PDF statements. But every financial calculation is deterministic code — no AI in the math. Excel and CSV statements bypass AI entirely with direct column mapping. Numbers you can audit and verify, every time.

Platform isolation

Each platform has its own parser, its own rules, its own configuration. A bug fix for Grubhub can never break DoorDash. Adding Clover didn't touch a single line of Toast code. In financial systems, isolation is a requirement.

CPA-first design

Every screen, every report, every download is designed for the person who reviews the numbers — your CPA. Side-by-side views, audit trail descriptions, professional Excel exports, CDTFA filing worksheets. Built for trust.
